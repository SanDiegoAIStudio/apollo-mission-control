# Mission Phase Procedures

Standard operating procedures for each mission phase.

## Pre-Launch

1. All seats report GO status
2. Verify kOS connection
3. Verify telemetry stream
4. Flight Director: "Go/No-Go for launch"
5. Poll all seats
6. Flight Director: "We are GO for launch"

## Launch & Ascent

1. Stage ignition → liftoff
2. Gravity turn begins ~100 m/s
3. Stage separation as needed
4. FIDO monitors trajectory vs. profile
5. EECOM monitors power and consumables
6. Target: ~80km apoapsis before circularization

## Low Kerbin Orbit (LKO)

1. Circularization burn at apoapsis
2. FIDO confirms stable orbit
3. All seats check systems
4. Go/No-Go for TLI

## Trans-Lunar Injection (TLI)

1. FIDO calculates transfer window (phase angle to Mun)
2. FIDO plans departure burn
3. Flight approves burn parameters
4. Execute TLI burn
5. Verify trajectory intercepts Mun SOI

## Lunar Orbit Insertion (LOI)

1. Coast to Mun SOI
2. FIDO plans LOI burn (retrograde to capture)
3. Execute LOI burn
4. Circularize in low Mun orbit
5. Go/No-Go for descent

## Descent & Landing

1. Deorbit burn to lower periapsis
2. Powered descent initiation
3. CONTROL monitors descent rate and fuel
4. Touchdown

## Surface Operations

1. All seats verify post-landing state
2. Systems check
3. Surface activities (if any)
4. Go/No-Go for ascent

## Ascent & Rendezvous

1. Ascent burn to Mun orbit
2. Rendezvous with orbiter (if applicable)
3. Go/No-Go for TEI

## Trans-Earth Injection (TEI)

1. FIDO plans return trajectory
2. TEI burn
3. Coast to Kerbin SOI

## Reentry & Recovery

1. RETRO confirms reentry corridor
2. Service module separation (if applicable)
3. Atmospheric entry
4. Parachute deployment
5. Splashdown/landing
6. Mission complete
